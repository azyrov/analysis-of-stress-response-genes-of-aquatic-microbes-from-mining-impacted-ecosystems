# Purpose
Analyzing whole genome assemblies of heavy metal-resistant microbes.

## Real-word Implementation
Parsing genomic data of microbes with the heavy metal-resistant gene across `spatial` and `temporal` differences to determine the effects of the heavy metals used in illegal mining (also called `galamsey' in Ghana) on the ecosystem.

### Spatial
- Microbes from *upstream* locations to the galamasey sites are used as *control groups*
- Microbes from *downstream* locations are usedas *treatment groups*
